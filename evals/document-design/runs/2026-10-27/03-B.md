# Pause or resume a Harbor nightly digest

Use this procedure in the internal Digest console. Only the on-call operator can change these controls. Harbor sends one nightly digest per workspace; all console times are UTC.

Pausing prevents the next digest job from starting. It cannot stop a Running job, recall email, or undo sent messages. Paused workspaces do not queue missed digests.

## Pause

1. Confirm that an incident ticket exists. Open the workspace in the Digest console and match the numeric workspace ID at the top against the ticket. Similar workspace names exist; do not rely on the name alone.
2. Check the current job in the Jobs tab:
   - **Pending:** the job is eligible for a pause. Continue below.
   - **Running:** do not use Pause to try to stop it. Page the incident commander with the workspace ID and job ID; the commander decides what to do.
   - **Completed or Failed:** the current job cannot be undone. You can still pause to prevent the next scheduled job.
3. Select Pause, enter the incident ticket number, and select Confirm pause.
4. Refresh the workspace's Schedule tab and confirm `Paused`. The Jobs tab alone does not prove the workspace is paused. If Schedule does not show `Paused`, stop and page the incident commander. Do not repeatedly toggle the control.
5. Record the pause time, workspace ID, and observed schedule state in the incident ticket.

## Resume

Resume only after the incident commander approves in the incident ticket. Resuming schedules the next run for the next nightly time shown for that workspace; it does not send a catch-up digest. The nightly hour varies by workspace.

1. Open the workspace in the Digest console and match its numeric ID at the top against the incident ticket before making a change.
2. On the Schedule tab, select Resume, then Confirm resume.
3. Refresh and confirm `Active` and the next UTC run time. If Schedule does not show `Active`, stop and page the incident commander.
4. Record the resume time, workspace ID, and observed schedule state in the incident ticket.

Manual send is a separate privileged operation outside this procedure.
