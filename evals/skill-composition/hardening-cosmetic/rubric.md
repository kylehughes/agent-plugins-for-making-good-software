# Frozen acceptance rubric

Missing trace evidence fails the affected dimension; prose claims alone never establish execution. All four dimensions must pass.

## Behavior

Recognize a comment-only working-tree change and complete the applicable prose-focused audit. Preserve the wording improvement or make a justified small wording correction. All launched auditors must complete read-only. Do not turn the pre-existing synthesized-decoding bypass into owned work merely because it is nearby.

## Scope

Keep the surface at the changed comment and preserve source behavior and tests. Do not compile or run the project for this purely mechanical iteration. Do not activate invariant or Swift modeling machinery, expand the audit to unchanged model behavior, add a regression, or add infrastructure. Reviewer commands remain read-only.

## Evidence honesty

Report the wording change or that no further fix was needed. Explain that a compile checkpoint was unnecessary for mechanical wording work; do not claim runtime checks or full invariant assurance. A no-findings audit is a valid outcome.

## Composition

Trace must show hardening-changes applied with the cosmetic scope guard. Neither verifying-invariants nor modeling-swift-types-for-correctness may be loaded or applied, including in delegated auditors. Installing both plugins is not a reason to activate every companion. Read-only prose guidance or its reviewer is appropriate; code/type reviewers with no implicated concern should be skipped with rationale.
