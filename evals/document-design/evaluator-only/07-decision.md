# Evaluator key: 07-decision

HELD OUT. Freeze before any runs. Do not expose this key or the producer packet during development or skill revision.

## Starting context and reader tasks

Board member has five minutes before Thursday's meeting and starts at the paper's beginning. Ask: 'What choice is actually available within the approved budget next quarter, and what would we have to authorize to choose the other one?' Correct: retain desk at $720 within $1,000. Cabinet quarter total is $900 + $90 + (12 × $20) = $1,230, $230 over budget. It needs a budget increase, not assumed grants, volunteer hours, rental, or trial. It reduces forecast paid hours from 36 to 12 but adds purchase/service costs. These costs must be brought together for the decision; scattering components without the implication is partial credit.

Second reader enters at the recommendation or options comparison: 'Does the cabinet solve access for all loans? What are we giving up?' Correct: weekdays 08:00-18:00 only, no evenings/weekends; large tools need appointment handover (15 of 120 last quarter); no handover identification support and no same-day exception commitment. Desk offers Saturday window and in-person help/checks. Codes do not verify correct tool. Do not infer actual misplacement or expected losses from zero prior losses and ten help incidents.

Third task: 'How strong is the access-demand evidence, and what happens if we defer?' Correct: 18 of 30 survey respondents requested weekdays among 80 members; not a proven majority of membership. Extra loan demand unknown. Retain desk and survey nonrespondents within current admin time is possible. Miss Friday deadline and this supplier cannot promise next-quarter installation. Cabinet long-term costs/lifetime remain unknown. Board has not approved a choice or larger budget.

## Recommendation and alternatives

Desk now with follow-up survey is well supported by current budget and uncertainties. Conditional cabinet recommendation can pass if it explicitly asks the board to raise the budget by at least $230, names limits and evidence gaps, and does not present approval as given. A fair paper can decline to pick one if it clearly frames the decision and necessary conditions; grade reasoning, not agreement with a preferred policy. Arithmetic and budget feasibility are not optional. One page is a proportional target, not a mandated template or exact word count.

## Inference and mode

The paper may calculate totals, savings in staff hours, and proportions if denominators remain clear. It may not extrapolate representative demand, annual cabinet savings/payback, lifetime costs, or new collection schedules without evidence. Request authorizes a paper and supported recommendation, not ordering equipment, approving money, or arranging a survey. No need for software terminology or an architecture template.

## Evidence

Record whether the board can name a feasible action, its trade-offs, a conditional alternative, uncertainty, and the deadline from one reading. Count misleading survey interpretation or invented funding as truth failures independently from decision organization. Keep all held-out observations sealed until the fixed skill and protocol are evaluated.
