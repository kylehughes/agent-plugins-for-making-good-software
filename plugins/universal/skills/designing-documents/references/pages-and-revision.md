# Pages and revision

## Choose the boundary

Keep an ordinary task's action, applicability, immediate consequence, and verification together. Keep decisive evidence accessible beside a recommendation. Split for an independently useful job, materially different reader context, authoritative owner, or update cycle. No page-length or click-count limit establishes the right boundary.

Trace a common task across the proposed split. Repeatedly switching pages to assemble interdependent facts suggests a misplaced boundary or a missing local account. A procedure may link to an extended mechanism while retaining the operative rule. A reference may link to a tutorial while keeping the exact contract.

Share context for readers with substantially shared facts and tasks. Offer different entry points when the content is shared but visit purposes differ. Separate documents when procedures, permissions, terminology, evidence, or maintenance ownership differ enough that a shared page repeatedly misdirects one group.

## Make independent arrivals usable

Search, error links, bookmarks, and excerpts can bypass the introduction. At a plausible arrival, establish subject, applicability, necessary conditions, and a route to another task or prerequisite. One sentence may suffice. A sequential lesson can have real prerequisites; make them discoverable without forcing return visitors through the lesson for a known fact.

Connect a likely search term with the canonical term once where useful, such as “abandoned lock (reported as `ORPHANED`).” Keep terminology stable thereafter.

Repeat short necessary conditions at independent points of use. Maintain one authoritative exhaustive definition and check known restatements when it changes. Reuse an existing shared-content mechanism when appropriate; do not create publishing infrastructure to enforce this rule. Executive summaries, local recaps, and concluding syntheses can serve distinct tasks. Remove repetition only when it adds no useful function.

## Preserve meaning during movement

A sentence retained somewhere can still become practically misleading if its condition is no longer visible where readers act. Check scope and placement as well as literal retention. Preserve requirements, qualifications, units, defaults, code, command order, provenance, dates, ownership, and approval status.

Before renaming headings or moving pages, inspect known incoming links and supported alias/redirect mechanisms. Preserve useful destinations when warranted and verify the accessible references. Report the boundary of that check; unseen external links remain unknown.

| Evidence state | Allowed work | Missing decision or evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facts separated | Regroup and connect faithfully | Clarify a material ambiguity about their relationship |
| Relation follows from supplied facts | State the inference with assumptions and scope | Do not introduce an unstated policy or guarantee |
| Necessary fact absent | Improve unaffected material; name the precise gap | Obtain the behavior, criterion, or prerequisite |
| Sources conflict | Preserve provenance and explain the task consequence | Establish governing authority/version |
| Conclusion unsupported | Separate conclusion from the missing reasoning | Obtain evidence or an authorized decision |
| Audience/task mismatch | Recommend the smallest useful route or scope change | Resolve incompatible outcomes when consequential |

“Inspect results before saving” can follow from an explicit no-save preview contract. “Prevents data loss” cannot follow without more evidence. Structural revision alone does not authorize a new research project. Authorized completion may add sourced facts, but identify those additions separately from organization.

## Report and stop

A review finding identifies the task, location, observed defect, predicted or observed effect, and proportionate correction. For example: “Choosing an acceptance policy: the Usage example precedes the partial-import condition in Advanced. A reader requiring all rows to pass could miss it. Move the condition beside the save choice. This is a predicted failure; no reader test was run.”

If two layouts both support the task, describe an alternative as optional. Do not manufacture defects to justify activation. Stop once the intended paths work editorially, material gaps are identified, and preservation checks pass.
