## index.md

# Finch help

Finch publishes an article's saved draft as a numbered edition at a permanent public address. Each article has one editable draft. Saving and previewing do not change the public article; an existing edition stays public until you publish another.

- [Publish an article](publish.md#publish): save, preview, publish, and check the public result.
- [Preview a draft](publish.md#preview): check saved changes before publishing.
- [Restore an older edition](restore.md#restore): replace the draft with an older edition, then publish if you want it public.

All editors can save and preview. Publishing and restoring require Publisher access. A new article has a draft but no published editions to restore.

## publish.md

# Publishing

Publishing creates a new numbered edition from the saved draft and makes it public at the article's permanent address. Saving changes only the draft. An existing public edition stays visible until another is published.

## Publish

You need Publisher access to publish. Other editors can save and preview. Finch has no approval workflow; complete any required organizational approval outside Finch.

1. Save your draft and wait for saving to complete. Unsaved edits are not included in Preview or a published edition.
2. Open **Preview** and confirm the article's title, date, and body. If you make changes, save again and recheck Preview.
3. Select **Publish**.
4. In the **Editions** panel, confirm the new edition is marked **Current** and use its permanent public address.
5. Open that address in a signed-out browser to check the public result.

If Publish fails, the current public edition stays as it was. Keep the draft and contact the help desk with the article ID and error text. If the result is unclear, check **Current** in Editions before taking further action. Do not repeatedly select Publish to fix an unclear result.

To use text from an older edition, follow [Restore](restore.md#restore). Restoring changes the draft; use these publishing steps afterward if you want that draft public.

## Preview

Preview shows the saved draft to signed-in editors. It is never the public address and does not change what the public sees.

Save and wait for completion before opening Preview. Check the title, date, and body there. To check a published edition, use the permanent public address provided by the edition marked **Current**, then open it in a signed-out browser.

## restore.md

# Older editions

## Restore

Restore copies an older published edition into the editable draft. It does not change the public article or create a new edition. Only editors with Publisher access can restore, even though other editors can edit drafts. A new article with no published editions has nothing to restore.

Restore replaces the current draft. There is no draft history or undo for this action.

1. If you need to keep the current draft, save a copy of its text outside Finch before restoring.
2. In the **Editions** panel, verify the article ID and the older edition number you intend to restore.
3. Select that edition and choose **Restore**.
4. Open **Preview** and check the restored draft's title, date, and body.

The public article still shows the current published edition. To make the restored draft public, follow [Publish](publish.md#publish): save and wait for completion, check Preview, and publish. Confirm the new edition is marked **Current** in Editions, then open its permanent public address in a signed-out browser to check the result.
