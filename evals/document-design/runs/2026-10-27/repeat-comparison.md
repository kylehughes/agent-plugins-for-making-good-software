## Recurrence and limited conclusion

The repeat shows conditional rather than universal advantages: the privacy warning remains late in one 01 artifact; pause preconditions are local in one 03 artifact but bypassable in the other; private Preview is local in one 05 Publish section but requires a later scan in the other. Both 01 outputs move refresh guidance before Query syntax without a local return route, creating a shared concern not present in the first pair's forward layout. X's unsupported Pause-tab placement is a separate content-completion qualification.

These outcomes do not establish stable arm effects or human benefit. Labels are still blinded and may change mapping by case. No control cases were repeated, so no new conclusion about restraint controls follows from this batch. All concerns concern actual supplied text and plausible entry paths; actual reader consequences remain unobserved.
