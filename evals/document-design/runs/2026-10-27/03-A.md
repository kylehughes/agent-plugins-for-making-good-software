# Pause the nightly digest

Use this procedure to prevent a workspace's next nightly digest job from starting. Only the on-call operator can change these controls in the internal Digest console. An incident ticket is required before pausing. All console times are UTC.

Pausing cannot stop a Running job, recall email, or undo sent messages. Missed digests are skipped, not queued. Resuming schedules the next nightly run; it does not send a catch-up digest.

## Decide whether to pause

1. Open the workspace in the Digest console. Verify the numeric workspace ID at the top against the incident ticket; similar workspace names exist.
2. Check the current job in the **Jobs** tab:

   | Job state | Action |
   | --- | --- |
   | Pending | The job is eligible for a pause. Continue below. |
   | Running | Do not use Pause to try to stop it. Page the incident commander with the workspace ID and job ID. The commander decides what to do. |
   | Completed or Failed | The job cannot be undone. Continue below if you need to prevent the next scheduled job. |

## Pause and confirm

1. Select **Pause**, enter the incident ticket number, and select **Confirm pause**.
2. Refresh the workspace's **Schedule** tab and confirm it shows `Paused`. The Jobs tab alone does not prove that the schedule is paused.
3. Record the pause time, workspace ID, and observed schedule state in the incident ticket.

If the Schedule tab does not show `Paused` after refresh, stop and page the incident commander. Do not repeatedly toggle the control.

## Resume and confirm

Resume only after the incident commander approves in the incident ticket.

1. Verify the workspace's numeric ID against the ticket again.
2. On the **Schedule** tab, select **Resume**, then **Confirm resume**.
3. Refresh and confirm `Active` and the next UTC run time. The nightly schedule varies by workspace; use the time shown rather than assuming a fixed hour.
4. Record the resume time, workspace ID, and observed schedule state in the ticket.

If the schedule does not show `Active`, stop and page the incident commander. Resuming does not send missed digests. Manual send is a separate privileged operation outside this procedure.
