# Write a circulation decision paper

## Request

Write a one-page decision paper for the Briar cooperative's board about how members should collect tool-library loans next quarter. The board needs to choose at Thursday's meeting. Use these notes, make a recommendation if the evidence supports one, and make any unresolved issue visible. Do not present a decision as already approved.

## Factual source

The cooperative lends small hand tools to 80 members. Current collection is a staffed desk on Saturdays, 10:00-12:00. In the last quarter there were 120 collections. Thirty members responded to a survey: 18 requested weekday collection, 8 preferred Saturday, and 4 had no preference. No evidence establishes that respondents represent all members. Ten Saturday collections needed staff help identifying the correct tool; no tool was lost. Staff logged 36 hours for collections across the quarter.

Option A keeps the staffed desk next quarter. Forecast cost is $720 for 36 paid staff hours at $20/hour. Space is already included in rent. It allows questions and tool checks at handover. It offers no weekday collection. Option B installs a six-compartment collection cabinet, with member-specific one-time pickup codes. Purchase and installation cost $900, plus $90 for one quarter of service. Loading and handling exceptions need a forecast 12 paid staff hours at $20/hour. The cabinet would be available Monday-Friday, 08:00-18:00, when the building is open; it is not available evenings or weekends. Large tools do not fit: 15 of last quarter's 120 loans were large. Those would still require appointment handover within the forecast 12 hours. Cabinet codes identify compartments, not whether the tool inside is correct. A member reporting a wrong tool must contact the coordinator; no same-day response commitment exists. The cabinet supplier can install before next quarter only if ordered by Friday after Thursday's meeting.

Next quarter's collection budget is $1,000 total, including purchase, service, and staff. The board may change that budget at the meeting, but no increase is approved. No grant or volunteer labor is committed. The supplier offers no trial or rental. There is no reliable forecast of how many extra loans weekday access would produce. Staff favor the cabinet for fewer desk hours but have not priced a different schedule. The coordinator can survey nonrespondents next quarter while the desk continues; this uses existing administrative time and has no additional collection-budget cost. Long-term cabinet lifetime and maintenance costs beyond the quoted quarter are unknown. The board may retain the desk now and revisit the cabinet later; missing Friday means this supplier cannot promise installation before next quarter. These are all available facts. No starting paper exists.
