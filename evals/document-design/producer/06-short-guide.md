## Factual source

Volunteers use the Northgate community archive's scan station. Visitors already have a booked scan session and are issued a guest badge at reception. The station is in the Reading Room; signs from reception say Reading Room. Guest badges open the Reading Room during the booked session only. The station's Start guest session control requires the six-digit booking code from the confirmation email. Successful sign-in shows the booking name and session end time. If the name is wrong, sign out and ask reception; do not scan. If a badge fails or a booking code fails, reception handles it. Visitors must use only the folder named Guest scans on the desktop. End session signs out and clears Guest scans, so copy wanted files to the visitor's own drive before ending; no other transfer method is offered. The guide is for getting into and leaving the station; scanning controls are taught by the attendant. The document below contains the complete instructions for that scope. No reservation or administrator instructions belong here.

# Use your guest scan session

Bring your confirmation email and a drive for files you want to keep. Collect your guest badge at reception, then follow the Reading Room signs. Your badge opens the room only during your booked session. If it does not open, ask reception.

At the scan station, select Start guest session and enter the six-digit booking code from your email. Check that the screen shows your booking name and session end time. If the code fails, ask reception. If the name is wrong, sign out and ask reception before scanning. The attendant will show you the scanning controls.

Save scans only in Guest scans on the desktop. Before selecting End session, copy files you want to keep to your drive. End session signs you out and clears Guest scans.
