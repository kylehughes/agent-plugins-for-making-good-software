# Restructure an incomplete access-change guide

## Request

This guide is hard for department coordinators to use when moving a case folder to another team. Reorganize it so they can tell what to do and what still needs an answer. We cannot get more information today. Use only the supplied notes, keep useful content, and do not silently settle disagreements between them. Return a revised guide and a short list of questions for the owner.

## Factual source

These are the complete available internal notes for Sable, a case-folder service. No external product facts apply.

Product overview, approved 6 September: a folder belongs to exactly one team. Coordinators may request a team transfer; they cannot approve their own request. The receiving team's manager approves. A pending request does not change team ownership. Completed transfers change the team and remove the old team's inherited access. Explicit individual grants are separate and remain until removed individually. A transfer does not copy or delete case contents. Existing folder links remain the same. Coordinators must check the destination team against the case assignment before requesting a transfer. The document does not say how to inspect explicit grants or who can remove them.

Support note, 12 September, author not recorded: go to Folder settings, choose Move team, select a receiving team, and submit. A banner says Pending approval. The note says a transfer can be canceled from the same panel while pending. It says transfer completion appears in Activity. It does not describe the permission required to cancel or what happens if approval and cancellation happen together.

Training slide, 18 September, marked draft: says receiving managers approve in Team inbox. It says the old team keeps access for 24 hours after approval. It says moving folders can be undone by choosing Undo in Activity, without describing who can do so or how long Undo is available. These statements have not been reconciled with the approved overview. A later date does not establish authority. No note provides a guaranteed completion time or a recovery procedure after a completed transfer.

Coordinator interview, 20 September: coordinators usually enter the guide at the old heading 'After approval' from a help-desk link. They need to know who can still see a folder, how to confirm where it now belongs, and what to do if the wrong team was chosen. The help desk accepts an escalation containing folder ID, intended team, observed team, and whether the request is still pending. It must not include case contents. The folder header displays current owning team and folder ID. Activity records transfer completion. No other interface facts are available.

## Starting move-team.md

# Moving folders

Moving folders is a flexible process for keeping work organized. Go to Settings and move the folder. Your files will be moved to the new team.

## After approval

The old team can still see everything for a day. If the wrong team was selected, use Undo. Check Activity when you are done.

## Other details

Managers approve requests. Cancel if you change your mind. Individual sharing is another feature. Contact the help desk when needed.
