# Research rationale and limits

This skill implements a provisional synthesis from the user-supplied **Document Design for Understanding and Use**, dated 27 October 2026. It does not establish that the skill improves human comprehension or adds value over existing writing guidance. The source-inspection limits below are inherited from that report; this implementation did not repeat its literature review.

## What the method combines

Reader-centered document design combines organization (what belongs together), coherence (how claims connect), instructional sequencing (what must be available first), and navigation (how a visitor reaches an answer). These overlap but are not interchangeable. More headings cannot supply unsupported reasoning; connective words cannot make a relationship true.

The five-stage agent method combines task/purpose, necessary relationships and prerequisites, and access/local context. Its six reader-context questions and two walkthroughs are proposed operating rules, not validated psychological measurements. No working-memory heading limit, learning-style category, or universal beginner-to-expert outline follows from the cited research.

## Evidence needed for adoption claims

Mechanical checks can establish local link resolution or literal changes, not semantic preservation. Editorial review predicts task failures. Model readers can expose contradictions or missing conditions, but cannot measure human attention, forgetting, effort, or retrieval time. Human sessions support observations about the tested people and conditions; a small formative round cannot establish a general effect.

The supplied evaluation draws on [GOV.UK task design](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing), Digital.gov's [paraphrase testing](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/test/paraphrase-testing), [usability testing](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/test/usability-testing), and [comparative studies](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/test/comparative-studies/). These are professional practices. [Zheng et al. (2023)](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.05685) motivates blinded, order-swapped model judging; its position and verbosity results concern the evaluated systems, not every later judge.

Compare against the actual competent baseline, preserve independent answer keys, include good short and reference controls, and distinguish organization from authorized content completion. Record added reasoning, facts, length, and navigation changes. A full-method comparison cannot attribute gains to the reader-context step alone; that requires an ablation. Retire held-out status once a case informs tuning.

Meaning preservation, scope restraint, condition placement, and honest evidence labels are safeguards. The incremental value, cost, medium coverage, and ideal amount of support remain open questions to test on independent topics and intended readers.
